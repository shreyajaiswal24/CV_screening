BASELINE ARM B — plain ChatGPT / plain LLM, no system around it.

This is the honest competitor. It is deliberately a GOOD prompt, not a strawman:
it asks for evidence and for gaps to be flagged. The point of the comparison is
not that a chat model writes worse assessments — it is that nothing checks them.

Paste this, then paste the CV text underneath it, one CV at a time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
I am screening candidates for an AI Engineer role. My criteria are:

1. Proficient in Python for building real applications, not only coursework   [REQUIRED]
2. Hands-on experience building with large language models — RAG, agents,
   prompt engineering, embeddings, or fine-tuning                             [REQUIRED]
3. Has deployed an application to production where real users used it
4. Experience with a cloud platform (AWS, GCP, Azure) or containers
5. Based in India, or able to work Indian Standard Time hours

For each criterion tell me whether the candidate meets it, quote the line from
the CV that proves it, and say if the CV does not mention it. Then give me an
overall verdict and what I should do next.

Here is the CV:
[PASTE CV TEXT]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT TO RECORD FOR EACH CV
  - wall-clock time from paste to usable answer (include your own re-reading)
  - for each of the 5 criteria: was the status right? (check against the CV)
  - QUOTE CHECK: search the CV for each quote it gave. Is it there, verbatim?
  - GAP CHECK: for anything the CV genuinely does not state, did it say so,
    or did it infer an answer?
  - could you paste 12 of these side by side and compare them?
  - is there any record of this decision a week from now?

The last two are not about accuracy. They are the difference between a chat
answer and a system, and they are where the case for building anything at all
actually lives.
